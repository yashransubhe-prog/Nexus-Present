## Overview

**Nexus Present** is a presentation-focused application concept under **Nexus Dynamics**, centered on turning ideas and content into visually polished slides through an AI-assisted workflow.

The product emphasizes a clean interface, design control, and convenient document-sharing experiences. This repository documents the product's vision, technical direction, and public-facing presentation without exposing its implementation.

> **Repository type:** Public product showcase. The Flutter application code, environment configuration, and internal integrations are **not distributed here**.

## Product pillars

| | Experience | Focus |
|:--:|---|---|
| ✦ | **AI-assisted creation** | Help users move from a topic or initial content toward a presentation draft. |
| ◈ | **Visual-first editing** | Make typography, colors, and slide presentation central to the experience. |
| ▧ | **Media-friendly workflow** | Support image selection and content-rich presentation design. |
| ⇪ | **Document output** | Explore PDF generation and native sharing as presentation-delivery paths. |
| ⟡ | **Smooth interactions** | Prioritize fluid transitions and a refined Flutter interface. |

*These are product areas and intended workflows. The uploaded configuration files establish the technology choices, but do not independently verify every feature end to end.*

## Design philosophy

**Make the result feel considered, not generated.**

Nexus Present is shaped around a few product principles:

- **Clarity** — important actions should be easy to discover.
- **Presentation quality** — visual hierarchy matters as much as the words.
- **Customization** — users should be able to express their own style.
- **Simplicity** — advanced tools should not make basic tasks difficult.
- **Consistency** — screens, typography, and motion should feel like one product.
